    Plate I · The Movement Alphabet¶

    Ann Hutchinson Guest's fifteen prime actions — the classes from which, she claimed, all movement derives. This plate is for staring, not reading: the small words are prompts, not definitions. The thinking behind it lives in Building a gamut.

    Action itself¶

    ANY ACTION the wildcard — something, anything, happens
    STILLNESS a rest — with duration, level, shape

    The body changing shape¶

    FLEXION fold · curl · close in
    EXTENSION unfold · open · reach
    ROTATION turn · spiral · twist

    Moving through space¶

    TRAVELLING cross the floor · go
    DIRECTION gesture into a line of space

    Meeting the ground¶

    SUPPORT take weight · stand, sit, lie
    SPRING leave the ground

    Against gravity¶

    BALANCE hold the center · suspend
    FALLING give in to gravity

    Ways of defining a movement¶

    MOTION TOWARD defined by its approach
    MOTION AWAY defined by its departure
    DESTINATION defined by where it ends
    RELATING defined by its relationship

    Solid cards: the ten performable verb classes. Dashed cards: not performables — ways of specifying a movement (by approach, departure, endpoint, relationship) plus the wildcard. One notation-world caveat travels with the plate: every system in this family under-serves floorwork — if the floor matters to you, imagine each solid card lying down too.
